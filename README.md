Round-based tactical FPS in three.js. You and two KITE ally bots face four
ANVIL defenders in Meridian Terminal, a dusk transit station with three lanes
and two bomb sites (A Ticket Hall, B Platform). Plant or defuse the charge,
manage money across rounds, buy weapons and gear.

- Rifle VECT-9, shotgun MAUL-12, grenade FLARE-6, plate armor, ammo refills
- Bots that patrol, hold, rotate, plant, and defuse; fixed-step simulation
- First to 5 rounds, max 9; buy phase, bomb timer, match reset

Play it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
